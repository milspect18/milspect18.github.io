About

Services include:

Amelia Voice Control System

Amelia enhances cockpit operations with its sophisticated voice control system designed specifically for pilots. This innovative system assists with managing aircraft checklists in real-time, improving both safety and efficiency without requiring an internet connection. A portable and light-weight solution that offers compatibility with any existing aircraft headset and provides up to 12 hours of continuous use on a single charge.
